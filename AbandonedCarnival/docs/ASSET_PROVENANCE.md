# Asset provenance

## Geometry and generated surfaces
Carnival architectural assemblies, rides, props and vegetation were generated for this project by the included Python scripts. No paid marketplace model, ripped game asset, branded character or unverified third-party mesh is included. Original procedural textures under textures/ were made with make_textures.py and the additional included surface-generation scripts. Woven carousel canvas is generated by derive_canvas_surfaces.py; leaf and plush maps are original project textures. They are synthetic, not photographed scans.

## Actual CC0 scanned surface assets
The art-pass scene uses downloaded, archive-verified ambientCG 2K assets:
- Wood092: continuous timber grain, physical tile 0.8 x 0.8 m
- Asphalt033: road aggregate, physical tile 2.5 x 2.5 m
- Ground112: eroded gravel/rubble ground, physical tile dimension unspecified
- Metal063: grey metal surface, physical tile dimension unspecified

Official source pages, download URLs, author/provider, license evidence and map checksums are in source/realistic_asset_sources.json. These assets were published by ambientCG under CC0. The original license snapshot is retained in textures/realistic. Derived darkening, weathering, paint chips, corrosion and crack overlays are reproducible in source/derive_weathered_surfaces.py, with results under textures/derived. A derived texture is not represented as an untouched scan.

## Typeface
The art-pass signs use bundled DejaVu Serif Bold, from the system font package. Its reproduction and distribution notice is included in docs/DejaVu_Font_License.txt. The font is packed in the art-pass Blender source; its glyphs are not claimed to be original lettering design.

## User visual references
Five user-supplied carnival images guided zone choice, atmosphere and layout. They are not used as replacement geometry or redistributed as project textures.

## Research not integrated
source/texture_sources.json records earlier Poly Haven CC0 research. Its downloads returned HTTP 403 and no Poly Haven surface was integrated. Additional ambientCG candidates also returned HTTP 403 and were not acquired. The included source ledger distinguishes the four successful downloads from candidates.

## Scope
The project work is provided for editing and integration into the user's game. Check any later third-party additions and platform requirements separately. This record does not promise exclusivity or legal clearance for independently added material.

## Streamlined texture delivery
The supplied maps retain source-render and rebuild inputs. Unused vendor scene files and alternate NormalDX/displacement/ambient-occlusion maps are omitted from the curated package; the acquisition ledger may list their original archive entries. Unreal materials use the supplied NormalGL maps with green-channel conversion. Original downloaded archives remain separate from this delivery.
