# Evergreen Market: premium layout proposal v1

This is an approval-stage floor plan and architectural blockout, not the finished store or an Unreal import package. The old Evergreen deliverables remain separate and unchanged.

Confirmed brief: one spacious floor; Evergreen Market; believable fictional brands; warm wood, pale stone and black metal; ten players; full grocery departments plus deli/cheese, florist and coffee/patisserie; interior-only gameplay with road/forest scenery outside.

Proposed envelope: 64 × 56 m (3,584 m²), roughly 70% larger than the earlier 44 × 48 m envelope. Approve the footprint, department positions and routes before detailed modeling.

Files:
- PREMIUM_FLOOR_PLAN_OPTION_A.png / .svg: dimensioned department zoning and circulation plan.
- DESIGN_PROPOSAL.md: layout, collision/export contract, performance and stage gates.
- Blockout .blend and preview images: coarse layout illustrations only. Materials, products, fixtures, lights, external scenery and detailed gameplay collision are not final.

Every eventual eligible placement must be an individual tagged StaticMeshActor in Unreal. Sharing mesh assets is allowed; converting only one representative per product is not. This proposal itself does not implement the import, collision or gameplay system.

Review questions: Is this overall size right? Are the café, produce, florist and service departments where you want them? Do the central aisle banks and connected warehouse/staff routes feel suitable? Detailed modeling starts after layout approval.

Toilets are explicitly reserved in the rear-right staff/utilities zone: customer access from the rear shopping loop and staff access from the service spine. The zoning images do not yet show final internal restroom partitions.
