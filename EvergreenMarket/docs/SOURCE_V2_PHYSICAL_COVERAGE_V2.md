## Important role exceptions

Promotional plinths and timber cabinet bodies share canonical meshes with signs and soil. Only explicit walking_instance_ids are enabled. Do not enable every instance merely because its asset has collider prototypes.

Fence rails are three separately placed single tubes, not one joined rail assembly. Each proxy encloses only its thin rail. Posts are independent. No fence-sized solid wall or gate fill is introduced. Bollards are two coaxial overlapping cylinders, body and reflective band; their 21 cm bounding square adds at most approximately 4.4 cm diagonal radial excess and does not bridge disconnected pieces. These are conservative simple proxies, not shape-tight convex certification.

Bakery cabinets have one base, three separate shelves and two side panels each; locker boxes cover the authored carcass rather than handles and labels. Entrance planter and rooftop mesh dimensions must be multiplied by placement scales exactly once.
